# Incident reliability analytics

MTTA is elapsed time from objective effective/open time to durable acknowledgement. MTTR means resolution time only; investigation, mitigation, and recovery durations retain their explicit names.

The production query boundary constructs Elasticsearch aggregations server-side. It uses `percentiles`, `avg`, `sum`, `filters`, and bounded `terms`; browsers cannot submit Elasticsearch DSL. Breakdowns are allowlisted to severity, priority, business service, and Data Product. Confirmed recurrence—not machine candidacy—is the recurrence KPI.

All compliance responses include eligible, met, breached, unknown, and excluded counts plus coverage. Unknown evidence is not silently classified as a failure. Lifecycle duration data remains sourced from the incident/PIR timeline.
