# Investigation evidence contract

Evidence retains capability, owner, effective/event time, distinct observation time, state, severity, entity reference, provenance, optional confidence, optional impact score, request ID, and canonical deep link. Provenance is one of measured, observed, derived, estimated, inferred, user action, system state, or unavailable. Confidence is omitted unless supplied. Estimates and future timestamps remain labelled; measured zero is retained. Missing, stale, partial, unavailable, empty, timed-out, permission-denied and unsupported states are not health claims.
