# Investigating asset trust

Read score, confidence, and coverage together. Start with an applied cap and negative factors, then inspect the
linked canonical SLO, monitor, contract, job, dbt, schema, lineage, or ownership record. A high observed score
with low confidence means evidence is incomplete, not that the asset is proven reliable. Missing monitoring,
contracts, lineage, or ownership should be addressed as coverage gaps. Compare immutable history and component
deltas; schema events are temporal correlations and must not be described as causes without further evidence.
