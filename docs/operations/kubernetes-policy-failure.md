# Kubernetes policy failure response

Quarantine the release; do not downgrade enforce to audit. Identify the policy/component from the redacted report, correct values or artifact digest, render again, and independently verify evidence checksums and SHA. For running drift, stop rollout and restore the declared digest/security context. Rotate a credential if literal-secret detection fires, even though reports contain only key fingerprints.

Emergency exceptions require the published schema, specific policy/component, approval, owner, expiry and compensating control. Expired exceptions fail. Escalate CNI/FQDN egress and ingress-controller annotation findings to the platform owner.
