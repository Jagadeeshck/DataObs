# Platform SLO operations

The YAML contract defines targets, not achievement. Detect burn with eligible events and completeness; confirm exclusions and clock window; mitigate the underlying serving or worker failure; verify fresh complete data; escalate exhausted budgets; retain inputs and calculation output. Missing or empty windows are `insufficient_data`. Never delete failing events, exclude 429/5xx ad hoc, or claim RPO/RTO from an unmeasured target.
