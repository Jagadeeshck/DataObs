# Global entity search

Press `/`, Ctrl+K or Cmd+K for Quick Find. Pages and scoped recents appear immediately; entering two characters searches supported entities incrementally. Use `/search` for provider filtering and provider status. Clear removes the in-memory query. Results navigate through canonical encoded routes. v1 excludes entities without an approved bounded server-side contract and does not offer fuzzy, semantic, payload, cross-tenant, saved or exported search.
