# External assurance crosswalk

DataObs security posture v1 does **not** assert ISO/IEC 27001 certification, SOC 2 compliance, NIST or CIS conformance, OWASP verification, PCI DSS compliance, FedRAMP authorization, regulatory approval, penetration-test certification, or auditor approval.

A future reviewed crosswalk may reference those frameworks. Any such mapping is informational/non-certification until reviewed by qualified control owners and supported by independently retained evidence. The internal `SEC-*` identifiers are product controls, not invented external-framework identifiers.
